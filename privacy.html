Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Inkfall ("the app") is developed and published by Razvan Popa ("we", "us"). Inkfall has no accounts and never asks for your name. To keep the game working, understand how it is played and answer your feedback, the app sends a small amount of data. This policy lists all of it. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising or to track you across other apps or websites.

Data we collect

A random app identifier. The app creates a random identifier on your device. The data below is tied to that identifier, not to your name.

Gameplay and usage events (FolioKit). The app records events such as "run started", "run completed" or "paywall shown", and how long a session lasts, together with the app version, iOS version, device model and approximate country. We use them to fix bugs, balance the game and decide what to build next. FolioKit is our own analytics service.

Feedback you send. If you send feedback from inside the app, we receive your message and, only if you choose to add it, your email address so we can reply. We use it only to read and answer your feedback.

Purchases (RevenueCat). The Full Scroll is bought through Apple. We use RevenueCat to confirm the purchase so the app can unlock and restore it. RevenueCat receives your purchase record under the random identifier, never your name or payment details.

Data we do not collect

Your name, contacts, location, photos or messages, and your device's advertising identifier. The app does not ask to track you.

Data stored on your device

Your gameplay progress, unlocks, settings, Ink Journal and run history are stored on your device (via Apple's standard on-device storage). Uninstalling the app removes this data.

Game Center

If you choose to sign in to Game Center for leaderboards, that sign-in and the scores you submit are handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy.

Payments

Payments are processed by Apple's App Store. We never receive or store your payment information.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep usage events and feedback only as long as they help us improve the game. To ask us to delete the data tied to your app, or a feedback message you sent, email us at the address below.

Children's privacy

Inkfall does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The data above is tied to a random identifier unless you choose to include your email address in feedback.

Changes to this policy

If what the app collects changes, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change.
